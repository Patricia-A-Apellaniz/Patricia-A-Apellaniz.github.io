What to notice

Try breaking it.

Use just 12 observations. Try a few seeds. Does the small group survive?
Then fit one Gaussian and look at the gaps between groups.

What’s actually running here?

A fixed, invented population with three groups. The first generator resamples observations with replacement and adds Gaussian noise (σ = 3.5 per axis). The second estimates one bivariate Gaussian from the sample. All calculations run locally in JavaScript; the displayed snippets call the functions used by this page. This is a teaching example, not a trained deep generative model or a privacy guarantee.
