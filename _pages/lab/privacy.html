What to notice

Try another angle.

Set copying to 100%. Then set it to 0% and reduce jitter to zero. What happens? Compare distances to training records and to independently sampled held-out records.

What’s actually running here?

Training points and 400 held-out reference points are sampled independently from the same two-component Gaussian mixture. The generator outputs 240 points: literal copies with the chosen probability, otherwise training points plus isotropic Gaussian jitter. Realized copy counts can differ from the requested percentage, and zero jitter produces copies regardless. Distances are Euclidean in these illustrative feature units; zero is tested within 1e−10. Held-out sample size is deliberately reported because nearest-neighbour distances depend on density and reference-set size. This is a copying diagnostic, not differential privacy, a membership-inference attack, or a privacy certificate.
