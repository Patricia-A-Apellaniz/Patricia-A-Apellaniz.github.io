SAMVAE combines clinical variables, molecular profiles, and histopathology images to estimate patient-specific survival curves in continuous time, including when several outcomes compete. The study evaluates the model on breast cancer and lower-grade glioma cohorts and compares its predictions with those of existing multimodal survival models.
